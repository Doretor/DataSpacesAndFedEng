Started with Copernicus Browser to simply visualize interested data.
First I look into near images, and it wasnt any spectacular.
After that i thought about analyzing data in time frame when last flood appeared (Poland 2024 Lewin Brzeski).
SENTINEL-2 within 01.09-21.09 was 1image for ~2/3days period. Sadly most interesting days (12.09-16.09) have
too may clouds to analyze them.
Images after flood (18.09, 21.09) have high quality information about flood efects, the same with images before.

Next checked provider was SENTINEL-1 with IW and VV+VH polarization.
SENTINEL-1 has significiantly more images ~1 per day. Sadly this images are worse than from SENTINEL-2.

SENTILEN-5P give us information about clouds / state of atmosphere which is not necessarly in our project.

Copernicus EMS was another option I checked. It is an on-demand service – authorized users must submit an official request to activate it during a crisis. 
Checking the September 2024 activations confirmed it is a perfect, immediate resource during the 'Response' phase, 
completely removing the need for manual image interpretation.

EUMETSAT offers incredible temporal resolution with new images every 15 minutes, clearly showing the massive storm system days in advance. 
While its spatial resolution is too low for local damage assessment, it is a crucial resource for the 'Early Warning' phase and evacuation planning.
